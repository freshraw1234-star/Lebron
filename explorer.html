GOAT Explorer

Search, Filter, and Compare All 5,160 NBA Careers Yourself

Don’t just take our word for it — test the numbers yourself! This exploration hub merges career regular-season totals with career playoff totals for every player in NBA history. We also compute a transparent All-Around Career Impact Score that weights postseason production twice as heavily as regular-season box-score production:

\[\text{Impact Score} = \frac{\text{PTS} + 1.5\,\text{REB} + 2\,\text{AST} + 3\,(\text{STL} + \text{BLK})}{1000} + \frac{\text{Playoff PTS} + 1.5\,\text{Playoff REB} + 2\,\text{Playoff AST}}{500}\]
